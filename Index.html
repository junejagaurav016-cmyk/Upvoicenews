Deepawali par Saharanpur ke bazaaron mein umdi bhari bheed, tyohari season ko lekar police-prashasan poori tarah mustaid

Saharanpur: Roshni ke pawan parv Deepawali ko lekar Saharanpur ke bazaaron mein zabardast raunak dekhne ko mil rahi hai...

Shehar ke ghantaghar, court road, raywala aur railway road jaise pramukh bazaaron mein kharidari karne ke liye logon ka tanta laga hua hai. Bazaaron mein saje diye, rang-birangi lighton aur pujan samagri ki dukaanon par kharidaaron ki bhari bheed umad padi hai.

Tyohari season mein umadne wali bhari bheed aur suraksha vyavastha ko dhyan mein rakhte hue jila prashasan aur police vibhag poori tarah mustaid nazar aa raha hai.
